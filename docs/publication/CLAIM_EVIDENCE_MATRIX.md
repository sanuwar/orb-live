## Claim-release rule

A claim may enter a manuscript only if its row is SUPPORTED, or if PARTIALLY SUPPORTED language reproduces the stated guardrail. UNSUPPORTED claims are prohibited. PROSPECTIVE claims remain protocol descriptions until future evidence is committed without retuning.
