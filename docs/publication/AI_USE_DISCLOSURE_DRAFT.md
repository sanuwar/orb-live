## Full methods disclosure

Generative-AI-assisted tools were used during the ORB-LIVE research program for software implementation, refactoring, test scaffolding, debugging support, repository inspection, structured evidence extraction, and drafting or revising technical documentation. The committed project record identifies Claude Code and Claude Sonnet 5 as the principal tools used during M43–M55; the Publication P1 audit and draft materials were additionally assisted by ChatGPT/Codex because Claude Code was unavailable in the P1 execution environment. Exact product/model identifiers and versions will be verified from retained records before publication.

The human author defined and approved the research questions, protocol boundaries, evaluation windows, classification rules, safety constraints, and go/no-go decisions; authorized empirical runs; reviewed corrections; reconciled numerical claims to committed artifacts; interpreted results; selected manuscript claims; and retains full responsibility for accuracy, originality, licensing, ethics, and conclusions. AI-generated or AI-assisted outputs were treated as untrusted until checked against code, tests, artifacts, and version history.

AI-assisted implementation and review did not eliminate defects. The project record preserves material defects involving calendars, universe propagation, market-feed selection, accounting, endpoint marking, warm-up leakage, concentration formulas, data availability, and safety checks, together with regression tests and corrected reruns. The study does not claim that AI assistance improved scientific validity or outperformed a human-only workflow.

No AI system is listed as an author. AI systems cannot approve the final manuscript, respond to accountability questions, manage conflicts of interest, or assume responsibility for the work.
