# Journal Target Matrix

**Policy check date:** 2026-08-24.
Only official journal or publisher pages are cited. Fees and policies can change and must be rechecked immediately before submission.

## Targets

| Tier | Venue / route | Reputation and scope fit | Article type and code/data expectations | AI policy | Public fee information | Major acceptance barrier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Realistic** | [Journal of Open Research Software — Issues in Research Software](https://openresearchsoftware.metajnl.com/about/submissions) | Strong fit for peer-reviewed experience reports on creating, maintaining, and evaluating reusable, sustainable open-source research software; official limit 3,000–4,000 words. | Experience report. | **Conditional realistic** | [Journal of Open Source Software](https://joss.theoj.org/about) | High-visibility research-software venue with public GitHub review and zero fees. | **Stretch** | [The Journal of Finance and Data Science](https://www.keaipublishing.com/en/journals/the-journal-of-finance-and-data-science/) | Official scope includes financial data science, econometrics, algorithmic trading, and finance software. Better finance fit than general journals, but expects rigorous theoretical/empirical contribution. | Original research or technical treatment; strong statistics, factor attribution, benchmarks, and reproducible data/code are expected in practice. | The [official author guide](https://www.keaipublishing.com/en/journals/the-journal-of-finance-and-data-science/guide-for-authors/) requires/encourages generative-AI disclosure under human oversight and prohibits AI authorship. | [Official OA page](https://www.keaipublishing.com/en/journals/the-journal-of-finance-and-data-science/open-access-journal/) lists **USD 700** excluding taxes for current submissions. | No completed independent validation, no inference/multiple-testing treatment, no factor regressions, and methodology novelty may be viewed as software-process rather than finance contribution. |
| **Stretch / practitioner-facing** | [The Journal of Financial Data Science](https://www.pm-research.com/content/iijjfds) | Official home emphasizes AI, machine learning, and data science applied to modern investment. The correction/multiple-testing story is relevant to practitioner research governance. | Article expectations and code/data policy must be confirmed with the editorial office; a rigorous finance-facing manuscript would still need the full empirical gap plan. | A current, explicit AI-author policy was not located on the accessible official journal pages during P1; obtain written confirmation before submission and use the fullest disclosure. | Current author charges were not clearly stated on accessible official pages; confirm directly before submission. | High empirical bar, current lack of prospective/independent evidence, and uncertain fit for a single-project experience report. |
| **Poor fit now** | [Quantitative Finance](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/rquf20/about-this-journal) | Reputable specialist venue whose official scope centers quantitative methods at the frontiers of finance. | A conventional quantitative-finance article requires validated finance/econometric novelty rather than primarily repository governance. | [Taylor & Francis AI policy](https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/editorial-policies/using-ai-in-your-research-and-manuscript-preparations/) requires methodological detail, tool/version, human checks, responsibility, terms-of-use confirmation, and prompt records on request; AI is not an author. | The accessible official journal page did not expose a fixed APC; standard versus optional-open-access pricing must be checked at submission. | Current record lacks significance tests, data-snooping correction, factor alpha, and independent validation; desk-rejection risk is high. |

## Recommended order

1. **JORS Issues in Research Software** after public-release/licensing and workflow formalization.
2. **MethodsX** if the paper can be written as a reusable protocol with validation steps.
3. **SoftwareX** if ORB-LIVE is packaged and openly released as research software.
4. **JOSS** only after all open-source requirements and the six-month public-history requirement are met.
5. Reconsider a finance/data-science venue only after the empirical gap plan and M55/independent evidence mature.

## Poor-fit warnings

- Do not target a top empirical-finance journal with the present point estimates.
- Do not submit a Software Metapaper that centers trading results; that route describes software and where to obtain it.
- Do not promise public raw market data until license review confirms redistribution rights.
- Do not choose a venue solely by impact metric; scope and evidence readiness dominate.
